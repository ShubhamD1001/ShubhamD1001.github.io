Capital Structure & Financial Performance Dashboard

Power BI · DAX · Power Query · Python (pandas)

Problem

Comparing how 226 large-cap companies are financed is near-impossible in a flat file with dozens of columns per company. Analysts couldn't see which firms were funded efficiently and which carried heavy debt against what they actually earn.

Approach

Cleaned the raw financials in Python — standardised currency to millions, flagged negative-equity and incomplete rows — then restructured the flat file into a star schema in Power Query (fact table plus company, industry and margin-stage dimensions). Built five DAX measures for leverage, weighted EBITDA margin, market D/E and capital efficiency, each wrapped in DIVIDE() to fail safely on zero denominators.

Outcome

One view showing the relationship between leverage and profitability: a scatter of leverage against capital efficiency, EBITDA margin ranked by industry, and a waterfall tracing gross margin down to net margin — which links interest costs directly back to capital structure. Full cleaning log published alongside the model.

Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

Power BI · MySQL · DAX · Power Query

Problem

Sales, product, order and payment data sat across seven normalised tables in a MySQL database. Anyone wanting to know which products earn their shelf space, or which customers the business actually depends on, had to write queries to find out.

Approach

Connected Power BI directly to the MySQL schema, modelled the tables into a star-schema relationship set, and built DAX measures for revenue, orders, customers and a zero-sales flag. Split the result into a three-page report — executive summary, sales & revenue, product & inventory — with cross-page navigation, global slicers and conditional formatting that flags low stock.

Outcome

Three findings the business could act on: revenue is heavily back-loaded, with November and December alone contributing about 36% of the year. Two customers account for roughly 15% of all payments across a base of 122 — real concentration risk. And inventory is misaligned with demand: 555K units sitting in stock against 106K sold annually, yet the fastest-moving products are among the thinnest stocked.

Predictive Lead Scoring Model

Python · Scikit-learn · Pandas — X Education case study

Problem

An online education company converted only about 38% of its leads. The sales team had no way to tell which of 9,240 leads were worth calling first, and the raw data ran to 37 columns with heavy missing values.

Approach

Dropped 21 columns above a 40% missing-value threshold, treated form non-responses as missing rather than as a category, and removed near-zero-variance flags with no predictive value. Encoded the remainder, scaled on the training split only to avoid leakage, and fitted a logistic regression that assigns each lead a 0–100 score.

Outcome

~91% accuracy and 0.97 ROC-AUC. Threshold analysis pointed to a cutoff of 65, where the expected conversion rate on contacted leads is around 80%. Time spent on the website turned out to be the strongest single signal — an insight the marketing team can act on independently of the model.

Inventory & Supplier Reliability Optimiser

Python · pandas · NumPy · SciPy · Tableau / Power BI

Problem

Stockouts come from two independent sources of uncertainty — customers ordering unpredictably, and suppliers delivering late. The common safety-stock formula ignores the second one, which systematically under-stocks whenever lead times are volatile.

Approach

Built a pipeline over 12,000 order lines implementing the dual-variability formula, so demand risk and supplier delivery risk are modelled separately and their variances added. Rebuilt the demand series daily with zero-demand days reinserted, so the standard deviation isn't biased downward. Service level is a single config value, so the whole model can be rerun at 90%, 95% or 99%.

Outcome

Safety stock, reorder point and a 2×2 supplier risk matrix per segment, exported BI-ready. The useful finding: for Furniture, most of the buffer exists because the supplier is unreliable, not because customers are unpredictable — which makes it a supplier conversation, not an inventory one.

Socio-Cultural Assimilation Research

Student researcher · Survey design · Interviews · Mixed-methods analysis

Problem

Students in University of Delhi English classrooms were disengaging, and nobody could say why. The suspicion was that culturally distant texts were alienating students from diverse linguistic backgrounds — but there was no evidence either way.

Approach

One of ten student researchers on an IQAC-funded innovation project led by Prof. Chhavi Sharma. Ran a pilot study first, then cut the student questionnaire from 35 questions to 14 after respondents flagged it as too long. Collected responses at scale by setting up a QR-code stall at the college gate, and interviewed 35 faculty members in 45-minute to 90-minute sessions. Analysed both quantitatively and qualitatively, then ran a classroom experiment with student-chosen topics.

Outcome

Data from 500+ students and 35 faculty. The sharpest finding was a contradiction: most teachers reported no problem with the curriculum, while 78.9% of students said they struggled to express their thoughts and 85.8% feared being judged on their English. 71.6% preferred texts by Indian authors. The classroom experiment moved previously silent students into active participation.

Ethics of Creativity in the Age of AI

Research paper · K.L. Mehta College national seminar, 2026

Problem

Generative AI now produces work that closely resembles human creativity, unsettling authorship, responsibility, cultural representation and creative labour — while risking bias, appropriation and decontextualised output.

Approach

Co-authored with Khyati (School of Business, Manav Rachna University) a paper introducing Moral Co-Creation — the argument that creativity is a moral practice rather than a purely technical or aesthetic one, and that human intention, interpretive judgement and accountability must stay in the loop when AI is a creative partner.

Outcome

Presented to an academic panel at the DGHE-approved National Seminar on Machine Learning, K.L. Mehta Dayanand College, and opened to questions from faculty. Presented, not yet published.
